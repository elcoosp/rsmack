Expansion benchmarks run with [criterion](https://github.com/bheisler/criterion.rs) 0.8.2 (mean of 100 samples, Rust 1.99, `ubuntu-latest`-class runners). Reproduce with:

<Code lang="shell" code={`cargo bench --workspace`} />

## Results

| Benchmark | Mean | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `utils/exec_env_build` | 37 ns | `ExecEnv::new` |
| `utils/finish_result_ok` | 161 ns | driver happy-path overhead |
| `utils/logr_collect_4_errors` | 4.1 µs | collect 4 diagnostics + flush |

| `megamac/exec_attr_generation` | 18 µs | warm, cached impl analysis |

## What the numbers buy you

- **The memoization win:** the 0.20 reference run measured `megamac/exec_*_generation` at about 1.35 ms per expansion. The impl-file memo brings warm expansions to 15–18 µs — roughly a **90× improvement**, most visible in rust-analyzer re-checks and test loops.
- **Shared constants files:** memoized consts save `N × 28 µs` for N structs annotating against one `src/consts.rs`.
- **Diagnostics are effectively free:** about 160 ns of driver overhead plus roughly 4 µs to collect and flush four errors.

<Aside kind="tip" title="Faster iteration in your own crate">
	Proc-macro recompiles get quicker when dependencies are pre-optimized. Drop this in your `Cargo.toml`:

<Code lang="toml" code={`  [profile.dev.package."*"]
  opt-level = 3`} />
</Aside>
